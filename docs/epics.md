# AI Engineering Workbench - Product Epics

## Vision

Build a collection of AI-native engineering capabilities that augment software engineering teams throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC). Each epic represents a major capability area that can evolve independently while contributing to a unified AI Engineering Workbench.

---

# Epic 1 – Requirements Engineering

## Business Problem

Business requirements are often ambiguous, incomplete, and inconsistent. Engineering teams spend significant time interpreting requirements before implementation.

## Product Goal

Help engineering teams transform business requirements into complete, actionable engineering artifacts.

## AI Concepts

- Prompt Engineering
- Structured Outputs
- Reasoning
- Evaluation

## Planned Features

- Requirements Analyzer
- Functional Requirement Extractor
- Non-functional Requirement Extractor
- Assumption Detector
- Ambiguity Analyzer
- Acceptance Criteria Generator
- Risk Analyzer

---

# Epic 2 – Architecture Engineering

- Long Context Processing

## Planned Features
